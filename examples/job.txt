Senior Frontend Engineer (sample job description)

- Comfortable with GraphQL APIs and CI/CD pipelines
- Clear written communication

Nice to have
- Experience with AWS or Kubernetes
- Built or maintained a design system
- Familiarity with D3 for data visualization
